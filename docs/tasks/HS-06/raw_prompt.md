# 06 — Hops and celebrations

After each round, players watch their bunnies move. This is the reward moment, so it should feel bouncy and alive.

Build:
1. Put the board into the game flow from task 04 in place of the plain score list (keep a compact score readout too).
2. After Confirm, each bunny that scored hops forward one stone at a time, with squash and stretch and a dust puff on landing. Bunnies move one after another, in a clear order.
3. Big round (5 or more points): a celebration, such as a spin with a burst of petals.
4. Storyteller scored 0: ears droop for a moment.
5. Winner: a victory dance at the finish while the others clap.
6. The camera follows the action, then settles back on the whole track.
7. A tap skips straight to the final positions.
8. If the phone asks for reduced motion, bunnies move without bouncing or effects.

Look at how task 05 built the scene and bunnies, and extend it rather than rebuilding it.

**Depends on:** Task 04 (setup and round entry), Task 05 (board and bunnies).
